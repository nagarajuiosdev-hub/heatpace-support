☀️💧 HeatPace: Heat & Hydration Coach

FAQ

How is the heat risk calculated? HeatPace uses the US National Weather Service heat index (temperature plus humidity) and the OSHA heat index risk levels. Work/rest, water and acclimatization advice follows NIOSH recommendations; training advice follows ACSM guidance.

Where does the weather come from? From Open-Meteo, using your approximate location.

Do I need an Apple Watch? No. With an Apple Watch, HeatPace can also warn you when your heart rate is high for the heat.

Break reminders aren't showing. Check Settings → Notifications → HeatPace, and turn off Focus / Do Not Disturb, which can silence alerts.

Is this medical advice? No. HeatPace gives general guidance and doesn't replace your employer's heat plan or a doctor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
